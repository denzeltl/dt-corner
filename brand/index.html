DT Corner brand

The mark is a corner of the evening sky seen through a window frame: the sun sets behind the hills as the day runs down toward the bedtime lockout. Full direction and tokens live in DESIGN.md.

Logo

Use logo.svg on night grounds, logo-inverse.svg on light grounds, and logo-mono.svg where only one colour can print (it takes currentColor). Keep clear space equal to the bracket's inset (about a quarter of the mark's height) on every side.

Mark and minimum size

The mark alone (mark.svg) is for app icons and avatars. Below 24px, use favicon.svg, which has a thicker bracket and a larger sun. Never go below 16px.
